## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal, editorial, and tactile luxury aesthetic specifically crafted for premium Vietnamese single-origin coffee. Drawing inspiration from high-end Vietnamese art periodicals, raw kraft packaging, and traditional relief printing, the system rejects standard e-commerce card grids and dark tech conventions in favor of open, magazine-grade spreads.

### Tone and Atmosphere
- **Warm Artisanal Heritage:** Grounded in Dalat mist, volcanic soil, and sun-dried coffee cherries. Evoked through warm ivory, tactile raw paper undertones, and generous negative space.
- **Editorial Distinction:** High-contrast headlines with poetic scale, precise micro-copy, and purposeful structural rules reminiscent of architectural press sheets and limited-run monographs.
- **Physical Weight & Craft:** Emphasis on product materiality—specifically the 500g physical bags (DELIVN Sand Ground Coffee and DELIVN Black Espresso)—framed like gallery sculptures rather than catalog thumbnails.

### Visual Style
A deliberate synthesis of **Warm Minimalism** and **Tactile Editorial**. Visual rhythm relies on sweeping white space, balanced typographic contrast, fine 0.5px hairline dividers, and deliberate bursts of terracotta lacquer red drawn directly from the packaging's star insignia.

## Colors

The palette operates on strict mathematical ratios to maintain warmth, restraint, and visual calm:

- **70% Light / Warm Canvas:**
  - `canvas-base`: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Ivory / Primary ground)
  - `canvas-subtle`: `#F5EFEB` (Milled Rice / Section alternation)
  - `canvas-surface`: `#FFFDF9` (Pure Bone / Highlighted tactile panels)
- **20% Sand & Kraft Texture:**
  - `sand-light`: `#E8DEC9` (Unbleached parchment / Subtle chips & borders)
  - `sand-medium`: `#D9C5AC` (Dalat highland loam / Accent fills)
  - `sand-deep`: `#C8B092` (Raw kraft paper / Structural accents & interactive secondary states)
- **10% Roasted Charcoal:**
  - `charcoal-pure`: `#161616` (Deep roasted Robusta / Primary headlines & high-contrast statement containers)
  - `charcoal-soft`: `#222222` (Secondary type & deep neutral borders)
  - `charcoal-muted`: `#5C5852` (Editorial captioning & body narrative)
- **Signature Accent:**
  - `terracotta-seal`: `#DE4833` (Red star heritage stamp / Badges, live indicators, callouts, and bespoke curation accents)

Use `terracotta-seal` sparingly as a focal punctuation mark. Charcoal backgrounds are reserved exclusively for deliberate contrasting anchor modules (e.g., the Delivn Black Espresso feature or origin story manifestos).

## Typography

The typography unites Vietnamese typographic cultural integrity with modern luxury publishing:

- **Primary Display & Headings (`Epilogue`):** Selected for its bold, sculpted glyphs that evoke linocut packaging prints and the bold weight of Vietnamese block stamps. Used for impactful declarations, origin tasting notes, and bold product naming.
- **Editorial Narrative & Interface (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Engineered natively for Vietnamese diacritics, ensuring tone marks retain optical balance at both micro and macro sizes without overlapping or breaking line heights.

### Typographic Principles
- All micro labels, index numbering (e.g., `01 / NGUỒN GỐC`, `500G THỰC`), and status chips must use `label-md` or `label-lg` rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).
- Long-form descriptions utilize relaxed vertical cadence (`lineHeight: 1.6` to `1.7`) in `body-md` and `body-lg` to create a meditative, unhurried reading experience.
- Maintain strict contrast against ivory grounds: headings always use `#161616` and running text uses `#5C5852`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an asymmetric, 12-column editorial grid calibrated to give individual product silhouettes physical prominence.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, max content width capped at `1400px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

### Compositional Rules
- **Gallery Asymmetry:** Do not place product bags into conventional boxed grids. Products span 5 to 7 columns with overlapping editorial annotations, technical coffee cupping notes (độ cao, độ rang, phương pháp sơ chế), and generous offsets.
- **Vertical Breathing Space:** Transition between narrative acts (e.g., from Dalat terroir to bag showcase) using `space-2xl` (7.5rem) to evoke the pacing of an art publication.
- **Structural Alignment:** All text groups, detail dividers, and secondary figures align flush to column guides; hairline borders stretch edge-to-edge across the canvas grid.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the artisanal print and kraft paper aesthetic, drop shadows and artificial blurred elevations are prohibited. Depth is established through physical layering, paper tones, and structural borders:

- **Tonal Grounding:** Overlapping surfaces using the tonal tiers `#FAF7F2` (base ivory), `#F5EFEB` (offset section), `#FFFDF9` (highlight sheet), and `#161616` (charcoal anchor container).
- **Hairline Tactility:** Surfaces are delineated with crisp, single-pixel hairline strokes using `#D9C5AC` (Warm Sand) or `#161616` with low opacity (`rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.12)`).
- **Physical Bag Staging:** Coffee bags cast soft, organic contact shadows created strictly with multi-stop radial tints reflecting natural ambient light: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(200, 176, 146, 0.15)`.
- **Packaging Framing:** Product photography sits uncontained or bounded by a strict 1px kraft rule, reinforcing the physical print sheet metaphor.

## Shapes

The shape system is crisp and architectural with subtle softness:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Standard for buttons, input wrappers, metadata chips, and editorial feature tiles. This mirrors the clean guillotine-cut edges of high-grade paper packaging without looking harsh.
- **Sharp Corners (0px):** Full-bleed hero split-screens, image masks, structural narrative drawers, and master section breaks.
- **Stamp & Circular Geometry:** The packaging star badge and origin certification marks use a true circular shape (`rounded-full`) or faceted circular stamps to reflect vintage lacquer seals and linocut stamps.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary CTA ("CHỌN GÓI NÀY" / "MUA NGAY"):** Background `#161616`, text `#FAF7F2`, border 1px solid `#161616`, `roundedness: 1`. Hover transitions to `#DE4833` background and border. Typography set to `label-md` with uppercase tracking.
- **Secondary / Terroir Action ("KHÁM PHÁ HƯƠNG VỊ"):** Background transparent, 1px border `#161616`, text `#161616`. Hover state fills with `#FAF7F2` on a soft `#E8DEC9` surface.
- **Star Accent Button:** Background `#DE4833`, text `#FFFDF9`, used exclusively for primary bag pre-orders or limited single-estate releases.

### Product Presentation Cards (500g Bag Units)
- Rejects generic e-commerce product cards. Instead, uses an open editorial layout:
  - High-resolution cutout of the physical bag (DELIVN Sand Ground Coffee or DELIVN Black Espresso) centered on an ivory or sand ground.
  - Linocut numbering in top left (`01 / BẢN NỀN CÁT` or `02 / BẢN ESPRESSO ĐEN`).
  - Terracotta star seal positioned top right as an authentic packaging guarantee badge.
  - Typographic specifications matrix below the image: Vùng trồng (Cầu Đất / Đắk Lắk), Độ cao (1,500m), Ghi chú hương vị (Hạt phỉ rang, chocolate đen, mật ong hoa cà phê).
  - Explicit physical bag weight indicator (`500G NGUYÊN BẢN`).

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- Background `#F5EFEB` with a fine 1px border `#D9C5AC`, text `#161616`, typography `label-sm`.
- Accent Variant (Specialty Micro-lot / Giới Hạn): Background `rgba(222, 72, 51, 0.08)`, border `1px solid #DE4833`, text `#DE4833`.

### Section Dividers & Indexing
- Spans full grid width using 1px stroke `#D9C5AC`.
- Integrated section index tags: e.g., `MỤC LỤC [ 02 ] — QUY TRÌNH RANG MỘC` aligned with the left margin in `label-sm`.

### Form Elements & Selectors (Grind & Quantity Options)
- **Input Fields (Họ và tên, Số điện thoại, Địa chỉ nhận hàng):** Transparent fill, bottom-border-only (1.5px solid `#D9C5AC`), active state shifts to `#161616`. Typography set to `body-md`. Placeholder in `#8C827A`.
- **Selector Tabs (Nguyên Hạt / Xay Pha Phin / Xay Espresso):** Pill-segmented container with `#F5EFEB` fill. Active pill is solid `#FFFDF9` with a subtle hairline outline and bold text; inactive option remains subdued `#5C5852`.

### Cupping & Tasting Note Matrix
- A horizontal multi-tier breakdown displaying roast depth, acidity, body, and sweetness with stylized minimalist sand-colored pip scales rather than tech dashboard sliders.
